Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Counter Pick respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect.

What we collect

We do not require accounts or collect personal information. The App does not store your summoner name, email, or login credentials.

Champion searches

When you search for a champion, your browser sends a request to our server. We do not log or store search history.

Third-party services

Champion icons and ability data are loaded from Riot's public Data Dragon CDN. Render.com hosts our server and may collect standard web server logs (IP address, browser type) for security and uptime.

Cookies

The App does not use tracking cookies. No advertising or analytics trackers are installed.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app developer through the GitHub repository or Riot Developer Portal product registration.

Changes

We may update this policy. The "last updated" date will reflect changes.
